Questions people ask before uploading

Is it legal to dispute my bill using Medicare rates?

Yes. Medicare rates are a public, government-published benchmark. Citing them to request an itemized review is a normal, lawful practice.

Will this affect my credit score?

Disputing a bill directly with a hospital's billing department typically pauses collections while the review is pending. Confirm directly with your hospital's billing office.

How long do hospitals usually take to respond?

The letter requests a written response within 30 days. The Pro plan includes a follow-up letter template if you don't hear back.

Is my personal and medical information kept private?

Your bill is processed to extract the data needed for your letter and isn't stored after your session ends.

Do I need a lawyer to use this?

No. CureMyBill generates a request for itemized review that you send yourself. It isn't legal advice.
