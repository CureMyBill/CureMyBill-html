1. Terms of Service

What CureMyBill is

CureMyBill is an automated document-assistance tool. You upload a copy of a US medical bill; we extract the billed line items, compare each one to the corresponding Medicare national reimbursement rate published by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), and generate a dispute letter you can send to the billing provider yourself.

Not medical or legal advice

CureMyBill does not provide medical advice, legal advice, or financial advice. The letters we generate are template correspondence based on publicly available Medicare rate data; they are not a guarantee of any specific outcome, discount, or bill reduction. Medicare's national rate is a public benchmark, not a legal ceiling on what a provider may charge. You are responsible for reviewing any generated letter before sending it, and for your own decisions about your medical bills, insurance, and finances.

Accuracy of extracted data

Bill data is extracted automatically using AI and may occasionally misread a code, amount, or name. Please check the extracted figures against your original bill before generating or sending a letter.

2. Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • The bill you upload (PDF or photo) — processed to extract billing data, then not retained beyond your session.
  • Information you type into the form (patient name, provider name/address, account number, your contact details) — used only to generate your letter.
  • Payment and email address — collected and processed by Paddle.com at checkout. We receive your email from Paddle to deliver your letter and receipt.
  • Basic technical data (IP address, browser type) — used only for security checks (bot protection via Cloudflare Turnstile) and is not sold or used for advertising.

How we use it

We use your information solely to: analyze your bill, generate your dispute letter, verify the listed provider against the public CMS NPI registry, process your payment, and email you your finished letter. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train AI models beyond the standard processing needed to generate your letter.

Third parties we rely on

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — reads and extracts data from your uploaded bill.
  • Paddle.com — processes payment and billing as our Merchant of Record.
  • Resend — delivers your letter by email.
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile) — verifies you're not a bot before we process your bill.
  • CMS NPI Registry — a free, public US government database we query to check the provider's listing.

Data retention

Your uploaded bill and extracted data are not stored beyond what's needed to complete your session and deliver your letter. Records of a completed purchase (needed for receipts, support, and legal/tax requirements) are retained as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, or to delete it, by emailing us (see Contact section below). We will respond within a reasonable time.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

3. Refund Policy

Because CureMyBill delivers a digital product (your generated letter) immediately upon payment, purchases are generally final once the letter has been generated and delivered.

That said, if something genuinely went wrong on our end — for example, a technical error prevented you from receiving your letter, or the letter was generated with clearly incorrect information due to a bug — email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll make it right, whether that's a corrected letter or a full refund.

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. We do not offer refunds simply because a hospital did not respond to, or did not agree with, your dispute letter — that outcome depends on the provider, not on CureMyBill.
